― COMPANY OVERVIEW

Connecting Communities, Enterprises & Technology Since 1999

Founded in 1999, South Bangla Computer has grown from an ambitious computer hardware specialist in Dhaka’s historic Elephant Road marketplace into one of Bangladesh’s leading nationwide authorized ICT distributors.

Today, we supply essential connectivity infrastructure to thousands of internet service providers (ISPs), system integrators, corporate enterprises, government agencies, and technology retail partners across all 64 administrative districts of Bangladesh.

As the Official Bangladesh Distributor for Meetion and an authorized partner for global networking leaders including Tenda, Cisco, HSGQ, Must, Solitine, and BDCOM, every product supplied through our distribution channel is 100% genuine and backed by factory warranty.
