Customer Assurance Policy

Returns & Replacement Terms

Applicable to retail orders placed through South Bangla Computers official channels.

1. 7-Day Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Replacement

If a newly purchased hardware device fails to power on or exhibits immediate manufacturing defects upon unboxing, South Bangla Computers offers an immediate brand-new replacement within 7 calendar days from the delivery date.

2. Mandatory Return Conditions

To be eligible for an immediate product replacement:

  • The product must be complete with original manufacturer packaging, user manuals, internal power adapters, and included cables.
  • The original paper receipt or electronic invoice reference must be provided.
  • The device chassis must be free of scratches, physical dents, or burn marks.
  • Defects must be verified by our certified branch technicians.

3. Non-Returnable Products

Items cut to custom lengths (e.g. bulk fiber optic drum cables cut per meter), opened consumable supplies (fast connectors, patch cords with broken bags), and items ordered specifically on non-stock corporate indent are non-returnable unless defective.

4. Refund & Exchange Settlement

If an identical replacement model is unavailable in national inventory, customers may choose:

  • An upgraded successor model by adjusting the price difference.
  • Store credit applied to their South Bangla Computers account.
  • A full refund remitted within 3–5 working days through original payment method (bKash/Nagad/Bank Transfer).
