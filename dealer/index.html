Dealer Partnership Inquiries

Who is eligible for wholesale dealer accounts?

Registered computer retail shops, nationwide and regional ISPs, certified network installers, and corporate IT system integrators holding a valid Bangladeshi trade license.

Is there a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Certain high-volume fast-moving lines (such as patch cords, standard ONUs, or unmanaged desktop switches) carry an MOQ between 5 and 20 units. Enterprise core routers and managed switches can be ordered as single units under dealer pricing.

How long does the verification review take?

Applications are typically reviewed within 1 to 2 business days. You can track your application status anytime using your assigned SBC reference number.
