South Bangla Computers Warranty & Service Policy

Last Updated: 21 October 2026 • Applicable across all 8 branches and authorized reseller channels in Bangladesh

1. Standard Warranty Coverage

Every hardware device distributed by South Bangla Computers is imported through official manufacturer channels and carries a genuine warranty. Typical coverage spans:

  • Meetion Gaming & Ergonomic Peripherals: 1 Year Official Direct Distributor Replacement Warranty.
  • Cisco Enterprise CBS Switches & Modules: 1 Year to 3 Years Authorized Enterprise Partner Warranty.
  • Tenda Consumer Routers & Range Extenders: 1 Year or 2 Years Official Replacement Warranty (model-dependent).
  • Tenda & TG-NET Enterprise PoE Switches: 2 Years Official Service & Parts Warranty.
  • HSGQ, BDCOM & V-SOL GPON/EPON Chassis OLTs: 1 Year Comprehensive Distributor Warranty.
  • Must Online UPS & Solar Inverters: 1 Year Standard Inverter Warranty • Battery coverage subject to vendor terms.
  • Passive Cabling & Fiber Accessories: Verification on delivery; passive cables carry no active circuit warranty.

2. Warranty Exclusions & Void Conditions

Warranty service will be declared null and void under the following conditions:

  • Physical impact, cracked casing, bent ports, or broken RJ45 / optical sockets.
  • Circuit burning, scorched traces, or blown capacitors caused by lightning strikes, external power surges, or incorrect voltage input.
  • Liquid spill, water immersion, rust, or oxidation of internal PCBs.
  • Tampered, torn, removed, or defaced South Bangla Computers serial barcode stickers.
  • Third-party unauthorized hardware repair, soldering, or unofficial third-party firmware flashing (bricked bootloaders).

3. RMA Submission & Processing Timeline

Customers and registered dealers may submit RMA claims through two authorized methods:

  1. Walk-in Service Drop-off: Visit any of our 8 showrooms in Dhaka, Chattogram, or Khulna with the defective unit and serial sticker intact.
  2. Courier Dispatch: Safely pack the unit and courier it to our Central Diagnostic Laboratory, Suite #830, Computer City Center (Multiplan), New Elephant Road, Dhaka-1205.

Standard laboratory turnaround: 48 to 72 business hours for initial diagnostics. If eligible for immediate swap, a replacement unit is issued from depot inventory.
